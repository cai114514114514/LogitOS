# Settings application icon

Regenerated at the user's request in the same flat, simplified style as
Preview. The original RGBA PNG is packaged as `/icons/settings.png` and used
by Dock, Finder and Settings. The earlier metallic icon prompt and screenshots
under `docs/design/settings` and `build-settings` document the superseded art.

## Generation prompt

Generate a finished FLAT MINIMAL raster app icon for Settings in LogitOS, matching a family whose Preview icon has a simple blue rounded-square tile and bold flat shapes. Square 1024x1024 with genuine transparent alpha outside the tile and at the rounded corners. Centered solid muted periwinkle-blue rounded square occupying 90 percent of the canvas with equal margins. In the center place ONE large warm ivory gear: eight broad, regular teeth, balanced simple geometry, a circular blue hole at its center. The gear should occupy about 62 percent of the tile width and be immediately recognizable at 24 pixels. Use only TWO solid colors, blue and warm ivory, plus transparency. Crisp clean 2D silhouette and smooth antialiasing. Absolutely no inner dial, small gear, sliders, tiny details, metal, gradients, bevels, depth, texture, shadows, shine, reflections, lighting, perspective, letters, text, emoji or presentation mockup. This is a restrained polished flat-design application icon, not a 3D object. Deliver a single finished PNG with real alpha transparency.
